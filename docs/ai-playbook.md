# Personal AI Coding Playbook

## 1. When I reach for AI first

- To get proposals for user stories
- To get proposals for skeleton


## 2. When I do not reach for AI

- To set the context of the application
- To set the objectives of the application


## 3. My non-negotiables

- I own what i inspect and approve
- I do not own what i did not inspect and did not approve
- AI suggests and I decide
- I do not share secrets or confidential files


## 4. My review rules

- Ask AI, inspect, run, test, refine: AI is a junior collaborator, it is useful. But i am the senior who decides on the final decision. Example: I had to correct the user stories.
-  Test after developping the backend: from data model, scaffold CRUD, business logic and API test: Use test to pass, then i break it to fail, then i restore it to pass. I had to check the business_rules to include the transitions in same status as valid.
- Test after developping step by step the frontend: the board, the modal form, the behavior refactor, the expandedn backend tests and debugging log. I had to check the form and ensure to have the "delete" button.



## 5. What I am still figuring out

- Understanding more the script of the backend
- Understanding more the script of the frontend
- Understanding more about containers

## Decision Card

- For a new feature I reach for: Cursor IDE, VS code and Copilot or Claude	
- For a code review I reach for: Cursor IDE, Claude, 
- For debugging I reach for: Claude Code, Cursor IDE
- For infrastructure I reach for:  Claude Code, and Codex
- I will never paste into an AI tool: secrets and confidentials
- My one rule is: AI suggests and I decide
- I commit to reread the playbook in 30 days
